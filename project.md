## Electrical Notes (IMPORTANT — Part P)

- Any new circuit added to a consumer unit requires Part P Building Regulations notification
- Contact local building control BEFORE starting electrical work
- DIY electrical work to a new circuit: either use a Part P registered electrician, OR self-certify (if competent person) via local authority building control
- Outbuilding fusebox: check it has spare capacity and RCD protection
- **Spec:** 2.5mm² 3-core SWA armoured cable, 20mm steel or MDPE conduit, IP65 fittings throughout
- **Burial depth:** 450mm minimum in soft ground, any depth under paving subbase is acceptable

## Materials Specification

### Natural Stone Paving

- **Area:** ~50 sqm + 10% wastage = ~55 sqm to order
- **Spec:** Tumbled granite setts OR Kandla Grey sandstone, 50mm thick, F2 frost-rated, R11+ slip
- **Laying spec:** 150mm MOT Type 1 subbase (compacted), 50mm sharp sand/cement mortar bed (1:5), full contact (no voids), flexible exterior pointing mortar for joints
- **Note:** Order all from one batch — natural stone varies between batches

### Pergola

- **Size:** 4×3m or 4×4m (confirm after measuring right-side zone)
- **Spec:** Powder-coated aluminium, anthracite, motorised louvres, integrated drainage gutters, base plates (not ground spikes)
- **Frame:** 100×100mm posts, 6063 aluminium, 60+ micron powder coat
- **Footings:** 4× C20 concrete pads, 450×450×700mm deep, post socket cast in. Pour BEFORE paving.

### Raised Planters

- 4× large: 1500–2000mm × 600–800mm × 400mm min depth
- **Material:** Corten steel (recommended) or hardwood oak
- Must have drainage holes + landscape fabric liner

### Lawn Edging

- EverEdge 3mm galvanised steel, 150mm deep
- ~30 linear metres (measure final curve before ordering)
- Set 5–10mm below finished turf level

### Lawn Seed (was turf — changed September 2026)

- **Area:** ~80 sqm (central + left areas)
- **Seed rate:** 40 g/m² = 3.2 kg required. **Order 4 kg** (surplus for spring 2027 patching)
- **Mix spec:** hard-wearing family blend, approx. 50% dwarf perennial ryegrass including
  regenerating (RPR) types, 30% strong creeping red fescue, 20% smooth-stalked meadow grass
  (*Poa pratensis*)
- **Why RPR:** ordinary ryegrass is a bunch grass — a worn patch stays worn. RPR spreads
  laterally and self-repairs scuffs from kids and play equipment
- **Avoid:** ornamental/"luxury" mixes dominated by fine fescue or browntop bent (won't take
  wear); cheap agricultural amenity ryegrass (coarse, tall, wrong cultivars)
- **Pre-seed fertiliser:** genuine pre-seeder formulation — low N, high phosphate
  (6:9:6, 8:12:8 or 9:7:7) at 35 g/m² = ~3 kg. NOT standard or autumn lawn feed.
  The high phosphate is specifically for root establishment
- **Sowing window:** seed down by end September. **Absolute cut-off mid-October** — below
  ~10°C soil temperature germination stalls and ungerminated seed mostly rots over winter.
  If missed, wait for late March 2027

### Climbing Plants (NWW facing = SE/S-SE opening — excellent sunny aspect)

- **Star Jasmine (Trachelospermum jasminoides) × 1–2** — strongly recommended for SE-facing. Evergreen, intensely fragrant, white star flowers June–Aug. Perfect for pergola.
- Clematis montana 'Alba' × 1 — fast coverage while jasmine establishes
- Hydrangea petiolaris × 1 — optional for back fence if shaded

### Outdoor Furniture

### Children's Swing Set

- EN71 certified, hot-dip galvanised + powder coat, blue steel

## Lawn Seeding Plan (September 2026)

Replaces the original June–July turf plan. Early-to-mid September in SE England is the best
lawn establishment window of the year: soil still ~16–18°C, reliable rain, weed pressure
collapsing, and eight months of root development before heavy summer use in 2027.

**Precondition:** all hard landscaping traffic across the centre/left lawn zone must be
finished. Seed will not survive barrow routes.

### Compost — use half of the 2000L

2000L over 80 m² is a 25 mm blanket. **Do not lay it as a blanket.** A pure compost surface
dries out fast at exactly the depth the seed sits, and settles into a soft, spongy,
scalp-prone surface later.

- Spread ~1000L (≈12 mm) and **rake or rotavate into the top 100–150 mm** so it becomes part
  of the soil profile
- Put the remaining ~1000L into the raised planters and the border in front of the
  outbuilding — worth far more there than under grass
- Check it doesn't smell of ammonia; if it does it isn't finished and needs weathering

### Sequence

| When | Work |
|---|---|
| Now → ~16 Sept | **Stale seedbed + fine level.** Rake to break the settled crust, work compost in, leave 10 days. Weed seeds germinate; hoe off flush. Free removal of most seedbed competition. |
| Same window | **Fine levelling.** Seed shows every dip that turf would bridge. Screed board or scaffold plank on string lines, fill hollows with screened topsoil, heel firm, cross-rake to fine tilth. |
| Same window | **Set EverEdge** 5–10 mm below intended finished grass level. |
| ~17–22 Sept | **Sow.** Fertiliser first, raked in. Split seed in half, sow in two passes at right angles (far more even than one pass). Rake in **very lightly — 5–10 mm**; buried seed does not come up. Tread or roll gently for seed-to-soil contact. |
| Weeks 1–3 | **Keep top 10 mm moist.** Light watering 1–2× daily *only* if there's a dry spell. Ryegrass shows 7–10 days, fescue 10–14, meadow grass 14–28 — a patchy week 2 is normal, not failure. |
| Mid–late Oct | **First cut** at 60–75 mm height, down to ~40 mm. Genuinely sharp blades, box on. A blunt rotary rips seedlings out by the roots. Possibly one more cut before growth stops. |
| April 2027 | **Overseed** thin patches with the leftover kilo. |

### Kids — the trade-off vs turf

Seed needs to be left alone. This is the one real cost of the change.

| Period | Access |
|---|---|
| Now → first cut | Completely off it |
| Spring 2027 | Light use only — walking across, not football |
| May–June 2027 | Normal use: paddling pool, swing set, the works |

Turf would have given a usable lawn in ~6 weeks. Seeding costs a season but gives a
better-rooted, better-matched, ~£300-cheaper lawn — and with the dog gone and summer 2027
as the target, the timing works in our favour.

### Topsoil — deferred deliberately

Buy topsoil only *after* the fine-level pass, when the actual dip volume is known. Two routes
depending on what levelling reveals:

- **Minor dips:** Verve Peat-free Top soil 25L at £4.50/bag, B&Q Sidcup, same-day collection.
  20 bags = 500L for £90.
- **Substantial fill needed:** Gardenscape Direct TS6 Blended Premium Topsoil **5mm**, 900 kg
  bulk bag, **£120 delivered** (DA postcode = £0 delivery surcharge). Screened to 5mm, BS3882,
  explicitly sold for fine lawn levelling. Their lorry has a **4m HIAB crane** — the driver can
  hover the bag over the target area and slit the base, so no barrowing. Rolawn and Earth Cycle
  are kerbside pallet drops by comparison.
- DA14 is inside the ULEZ but well outside the Congestion Charge zone; Gardenscape already
  prices DA at zero surcharge.

**Unverified — check before ordering:** Gardenscape publishes no lead time (phone 0800 854 663).
Rolawn's terms mention a temporary 6.5% fuel surcharge that may not be in displayed prices.

### Sourcing notes

- **Order seed + fertiliser in one Boston Seeds basket** — £42 goods, single £5.99 delivery.
- Runner-up seed: Lawnsmith EASYGREEN, a clean 50/50 dwarf rye / strong creeping red fescue
  with no bent, £34.90 + £7.50 delivery — but Lawnsmith warn of 3-working-day dispatch in the
  September peak.
- Rejected: Boston "Quality Hard Wearing" (£27) and Germinal Family Lawn (£12.45) are 80–95%
  ryegrass, too coarse-dominant. Pitchcare and Masterline have good 50/50 blends but 10 kg
  minimums.
- **No national chain stocks a true pre-seed NPK for same-day collection.** Bonemeal is the only
  stand-in and is a poor one (no potash, slow release, NPK inconsistently reported). Not needed
  until sowing day, so order properly.
- **Late-season watering stock is thin and real:** both B&Q clearance timers sold out; both
  mid-range Hozelock timers out of stock across all 8 Screwfix branches within 8.3 miles. The
  Titan at Screwfix Sidcup was the only programmable timer with confirmed same-day stock nearby.
- Homebase Sidcup closed Nov 2024. There is no Wickes in Sidcup or Bexleyheath.
